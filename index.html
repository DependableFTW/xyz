Our Story — A Poem for Ishika

"Written with all my heart — for Ishika"

Opening Reflection

We fight, we fall, we heal, we stay,
Yet why does love still drift away?
Each wound we mend, each tear we dry,
Still, storms return — do you wonder why?
We hold on tight, refuse to part,
But are we healing or just pressing restart?
Let's dive beneath the words we say,
And find the cracks that beg to stay.

CHAPTER I — THE TWELFTH GRADE GAZE

“Well, this story is about a person, whom I saw for the first time in my 12th standard...”

I saw her first in twelfth-grade light,
Drifting through desks, left and right.
Each one whispered, "She looks at me,"
I laughed — how strange could this all be?

Then my eyes met hers at last,
Deep and dark, so still, so vast.
No wonder all had felt her stare,
Her gaze was everywhere and nowhere.

I thought to tell her, share the jest,
But something held me, let it rest.
A moment gone, a chance slipped by,
And to this day, I don't know why.

CHAPTER II — THE FAREWELL PHOTOGRAPH

“Now a wise man is saying: 'Always expect the unexpected!'”

The days had passed, the time had flown,
A silent story left unknown.
Not once had words between us spun,
Yet fate had plans — its work undone.

With farewell near, I made a vow,
To step beyond my silence now.
A picture with all, a memory bright,
To leave with joy, not lost in night.

And then, as if the stars had schemed,
She stood before me like a dream.
One final chance, the very last day,
To cast my fears and words convey.

A timid "hello," a breath so deep,
A moment time had sworn to keep.
The shutter clicked, the echoes stayed,
A frozen frame the past had made.

Not just a picture, not just a glance,
But courage found in fleeting chance.
A chapter closed, yet hearts still know,
Some stories end, yet never go.

CHAPTER III — THE GLOWING SCREEN

“Sometimes which appears to be an END is not actually THE END...”

The end was not an end at all,
But where the stars began to call.
For in the night, when silence grew,
A message came soft, yet true.

Her name appeared, my heart took flight,
A whisper sent through glowing light.
She asked for a picture, a memory framed,
One that another hand had claimed.

I promised to send it, waited in vain,
For a photograph lost in a filter's reign.
When it arrived, not quite the same,
Colors shifted, yet fate still came.
Perhaps she ruined what past had drawn —
But in that blur, the future was born.

CHAPTER IV — ENDLESS NIGHTS & PLAYFUL DUELS

“Dramatic reference of our further story...”

From a single text to endless nights,
We talked and talked under moonlit lights.
Day by day, she felt less unknown,
A stranger once, now a bond had grown.

Most of our words held no real theme,
Yet I stretched each chat like a fragile dream.
Nonsense, laughter, playful fights,
Yet I clung to them with all my might.

I teased her, poked at her pride,
Said she's not a girl — just to see her fiery side.
Or dropped a name she might secretly admire,
Watching her rage, setting heart on fire.

She'd argue, she'd scowl, she'd call me a fool,
Yet somehow, I cherished each little duel.
I don't know why, I can't explain,
But talking to her feels like a home in the rain.

CHAPTER V — THE UNSPOKEN WEIGHT

“It feels so heavy whenever she talks about others...”

Maybe it's true, maybe it's real,
This growing weight I start to feel.
I never meant for this to be,
Yet here I am, lost at sea.

I tease, I joke, I play around,
Yet my heart beats loud, an unspoken sound.
I never told her, never tried,
That every time a part of me died.

Whenever she laughs, talking his name,
I smile, I nod, but it's not the same.
A quiet fire, a hidden sting,
A war within that words don't bring.

I hate it, yet I never show,
She'll never ask, she'll never know.
So I keep talking, hold her near,
Hiding truth behind a jeer.

CHAPTER VI — CROSSING THE LINE

“So this is how I confessed to her my feelings...”

Every guy in her life, a villain to me,
A shadow cast on the love I can't free.
I made a vow, silent and strong,
To bury my feelings, to prove myself wrong.

She sees me a friend, nothing more nothing less,
So I lock my heart, let it sink, suppress.
Yet on my birthday, her presence shone bright,
A moment so simple, yet it felt so right.

Deep inside, I made a decree,
To never hurt her, to let her be.
To guard her joy, to keep her safe,
Even if love meant my heart's own grave.

But then came a spark, a whisper, a sign,
Her friend's words nudged me to cross the line.
A feeling, a hint, a chance so slight,
That maybe, just maybe, she felt it right.

For once, I stood against my own tide,
Pushed past the walls where my fears reside.
And with a strength I rarely knew,
I confessed — heart open, raw and true.

Would I have dared, without divine grace?
Without Lord Krishna's guiding embrace?
Perhaps not, but fate had aligned,
A moment where love no longer confined.

CHAPTER VII — THE NOVEMBER STORM & THE TURN OF FATE

“Wondering what happened after I confessed? It's even more dramatic...”

I laid it all bare, no walls, no disguise,
Every thought, every feeling, no more lies.
But silence lingered, stretching so long,
Each second a battle, each moment felt wrong.

We talked still, as if nothing had changed,
Yet my heart knew — we weren't the same.
I got angry, confused, lost in the haze,
Why not just tell me and end these days?

Mixed signals, hope tangled with doubt,
One day she's near, the next she's out.
Then came November, cold and cruel,
A night that shattered my every rule.

She finally spoke, broke the thread,
With words that turned my heart to lead.
She didn't feel the way I did,
And in that moment, my soul slid.

Tears fell fast, blame hit deep,
I lost her twice — love and friendship to keep.
Two days of silence, empty and long,
I swore I was done, I had to be strong.

But then, like fate had flipped the page,
On the 4th, she returned to stage.
One simple line, one sudden light,
And somehow, she made my darkest night bright.

CHAPTER VIII — CLASHING, TRUSTING & GROWING

“How everything is going on...”

From that day on, everything felt right,
Though fights would come, like day and night.
At least once a month, we'd clash and collide,
Yet somehow, we'd always stand side by side.

No one's perfect, that much is true,
But through it all, I still trust you.
And I hope you trust me just the same,
Through every storm, through joy and pain.

I remember our first fight, your birthday night,
I thought you lied — maybe I wasn't right.
We were silly, don't you think?
Yet even then, we didn't sink.

Lies are common, they come and go,
But between us I want truth to flow.
No hiding, no secrets, nothing to fear,
That's how we'll grow, year by year.

Maybe I'm possessive, though I don't show,
But if you're mine, then the world should know.
I won't change, I'll stay the same,
I just hope you feel my flame.

We fight, yes, but that's okay,
As long as we find our way.
Through every fall, through every test,
Sorting it out — that's what makes us best.

CHAPTER IX — PHIR KYA HUA? HAND IN HAND

“Sorry for the delay in writing. Let's start... Phir? Phir Kya hua??”

The months rolled on, the seasons turned,
A steady fire inside us burned.
Through heavy books and endless grind,
You were the peace I sought to find.
No longer guessing where we stand,
Just walking closely, hand in hand.

Late-night talks and quiet grace,
Finding our home in every space.
You learned my flaws, I learned your ways,
Through busy nights and heavy days.
Real and raw, without a mask,
Loving you, never feels like a task.

A communication gap we didn't need,
Yet through the distance, love took heed.
Surrounded by your Mosi, Mama near,
With Vami right beside you, drawing clear,
You still carved out the time to talk to me,
I see the effort, rare and true,
And I'm so glad, and grateful, it was you.

Things didn't go the way they should have gone,
When your Didi left, our peace was gone.
You said you'd leave, your heart was deeply hurt,
And parting words began to stir and blur.
I'm sorry for the pain I caused you then,
Please, don't ever say those words again.
Through every fight, I want you right with me,
Just stay, and let our love run wild and free.

We really enjoyed the month we met,
November moments I won't forget.
On the 4th I said, "You have to come,"
And suddenly our hearts beat as one.
The strongest cake you gave me on my day,
And celebrating yours in our special way.
For the first time, walking hand in hand,
To the Gurudwara, just as unplanned.

CHAPTER X — COLLEGE GATES & 2025

“Leaving 2025 Behind...”

I really enjoyed our days back then,
Meeting outside your college gate again.
Taking you home, reluctant to say goodbye,
As 2025 slipped under the sky.
I wasn't ready to let that year depart,
Unaware of what 2026 held in its heart.

CHAPTER XI — THE FIRST ROSE & FIRST KISS

“~ Zoroko, Charisma, and the First Kiss...”

The very first day at Zoroko we met,
You were busy on your phone, ignoring me yet.
Sitting beside you, I gave you a rose,
Hope you liked it, despite how it shows.
Sorry for the little taunt I had to make,
A little tease for old times' sake.

Then came a surprise I hadn't planned at all —
A sudden kiss that made me softly fall.
Lost in that moment, I don't know how or when,
And into Charisma we stepped right then.
We enjoyed every day, a core memory so bright,
Carried through my life like a guiding light.
We've kissed many times, and I love them all,
But that very first one holds my heart in thrall.

CHAPTER XII — THE INDORE EVENINGS

“The Internship Days...”

I don't know how fast the days had passed,
Those internship weeks went by too fast.
Dropping you home was all I wanted then,
Wishing those rides wouldn't end.
Through every moment, crazy and true,
I really love you... my crazy love,
Yep, it's you.

CHAPTER XIII — FOREVER YOURS

“My Wish For You & Forever Yours...”

I wanted you to stay right here with me,
Though wishing for your dreams was meant to be.
I liked you close in Indore, yet felt your pain,
When colleges didn't grant what you hoped to gain.

I'm sorry, Ishika, for the times I lost my way,
Buried in audits when I should have had more to say.
I swear I'll make sure that it won't repeat,
Your heart and comfort make my life complete.

Whatever you wanted, I prayed you'd find,
Leaving all the heavy doubts behind.
Whatever I did, my truth stands clear —
I am yours, forever yours, year after year.

Another year, another test,
With you is where my heart finds rest.
From twelfth-grade shade to light so clear,
I look at you and find my year.
No matter what the world may say,
I chose you then... I choose today.

P.S. I Love You!! ❤️
